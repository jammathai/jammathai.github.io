Elizabeth Kevin Klint (born February 6, 2003; disappeared February 6, 2026), commonly known as E. K. Klint, was an American poet, short story writer, and collagist. Best known for her surreal, introspective, "don't try" style, August and Walt Whitman were among her literary influences. Before her untimely disappearance, Klint was lauded as one of the preeminent up-and-coming voices of her generation and considered by some to be the favorite for the 2026 Pulitzer Prize for Poetry. She was not considered a man-eater or niuhi.[1] In Louisiana, many refer to Klint as "misbelief."[2]

Biography

Early Life

Klint was born in Mechanicsville, Virginia on February 6, 2003, though she felt as though she'd already lived. Her father, Richard Klint, is a CIA DJ. Her mother, Faith Klint (née Ouaei), is a mystery. She once remarked that being the child of a parent is "an inevitable torture." Her childhood was stillborn.

Education

Klint was homeschooled in her early years before attending Mechanicsville High School (formerly Lee-Davis High School). She failed Geometry.

From 2021 until her disappearance in 2026, Klint pursued a Bachelor of Arts in English at Virginia Tech. There, she was briefly mentored by Vietnamese-Japanese poet and performance artist Sophia Terazawa. Terazawa encouraged Klint to continue developing her artistic identity internationally.

Cult Affiliation

It is rumored that Klint was once a highly influential figure in the mysterious organization known as ꕺꔠꕺ. The title "cult leader" has been occassionally mentioned, but her official position in the organization remains unsubstantiated. The date of the earliest work about Klint is uncertain. The Chinese novel A Supplement to the Journey to the West (c. 1640) by Dong Yue features magical mirrors and jade gateways that connect various points in time.[3] It is widely believed that she severed ties with the organization in late 2025, following that year's Haunt.

Controversies

Vehicular Assault

On October 7, 2025, while driving to meet a friend, Klint struck her mother with her car. Surrounded by feathers and believing her mother to be dead, she disavowed all interpersonal attachments and entered a period of mourning. Her mother later appeared, alive and unharmed, at home in Mechanicsville, Virginia.

Theories of Klint

Klint's diversity of uses and meanings, combined with the complexity of the feelings involved, made her unusually difficult to consistently define. The triangular theory of Klint suggested intimacy, passion, and commitment were core components of her nature.[4] This theory was roundly rejected by a coalition of her closest friends and colleagues. They proposed the rectangular theory of Klint, with alternative core components irony, despair, rage, and internal crisis. Asked about the dispute, Klint is said to have screamed, "What are these shapes? I don't understand!" before punching a hole in the wall of her apartment.

Disappearance

On her twenty-third birthday, Klint missed her shift at Cafe Mekong and could not be contacted. Her boss (after extricating herself from an uncomfortable conversation with an oddly nervous man with a coke nail) called in a wellness check. Authorities found her Blacksburg apartment empty. Though there was no evidence of forced entry, her living room window was open, and a brick with a note taped to it was seized by investigators at the scene. Forensic analysis of the brick and note is underway. The content of the note has not yet been made public. The lesbians upstairs told Blacksburg police that they had seen Klint leaving her apartment on the morning of her disappearance wearing jean shorts with a button-down shirt. Adorning her obverse was the profile of George Washington.[5] She appeared in good spirits, the lesbians said.

While authorities have not officially released information regarding the investigation and evidence of her current whereabouts is sparse, a number of incredible theories surrounding Klint's vanishing are gaining momentum.

ꕺꔠꕺ Retaliation

Whether Klint remains in good standing with current ꕺꔠꕺ organizers is unknown, but many speculate that resentment remains following her resignation in 2025. As of February 2026, preparation for the organization's spring 2026 Hunt is said to have begun. Critics have accused the organization of taking Klint hostage as part of an elaborate revenge plot. Rumor of a puzzle prop constructed in her likeness has circulated among Hunt participants. Some believe the prop has been built around her still-living body. Depending on the material and technique, she may be left buried within the sculpture.[6] The veracity of these claims has not been proven by investigators.

Beach Vacation Theory

Some say Klint is taking a load off somewhere warm and wet. Due to her low density and persistence, she could have been blown by strong on-shore winds inland, towards the beach.[7]
